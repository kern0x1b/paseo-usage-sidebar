# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/)
version 3.0. The summary below states how it applies here; the linked text governs anything it does
not cover.

## Scope

This applies to every space the project uses — the repository, issues, pull requests, and any
conversation where someone is representing the project.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful and direct. Assume the other person is acting in good faith and ask before concluding
otherwise. Accept feedback on your work, and give it on someone else's without making it about them.
When you get something wrong, fix it and say so.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks. Publishing someone's private information.
Sustained disruption of discussions. Sexualized language or attention. Conduct a reasonable person
would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report anything covered above to **157722763+kern0x1b@users.noreply.github.com**. Reports are read
by the maintainer only and are acknowledged within 3 business days. Include what happened, where,
and when — links or screenshots help. Reporting in good faith never counts against you, and the
identity of the reporter is not shared with the person reported.

## Consequences

Responses follow the four-step ladder in Contributor Covenant 3.0: a private warning, a temporary
restriction on participation, a temporary suspension, and a permanent ban. Which step applies
depends on severity and on whether the behaviour continues.
